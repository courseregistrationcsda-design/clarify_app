# Welcome to Clarify 🚀

Clarify is your personal, AI-powered meeting companion. It is designed to take the stress out of back-to-back meetings by capturing your audio, structuring your notes, and answering questions about your past meetings—all securely in your browser.

Here is a quick-start guide to mastering your new workspace.

---

## 1. Getting Started: The Dashboard
When you log in, you will see your **Bento Dashboard**. 
* **Pulse & Tasks:** These cards automatically track how much time you spend in meetings and what percentage of your action items are completed.
* **Integrations:** Clarify natively pushes tasks to tools like Linear and Slack.

To begin, click the big **New Meeting** button.

---

## 2. Running a Meeting
Instead of starting from a blank page, use the **Template Dropdown** when creating a meeting (e.g., *1:1 Check-in* or *Client Discovery*). Clarify will instantly build your agenda on the left side of the screen.

### 🎙️ Capturing Audio (Bot-Free)
You do not need to invite an awkward AI bot to your Zoom calls anymore. 
*   **The "Listen" Button (Top Bar):** Click this to activate your computer's microphone. Clarify will dictate your voice directly into the notes in real-time.
*   **The "Record Tab" Button:** If you are on a web-based call (like Google Meet), click this and select your meeting tab. Clarify will securely intercept the audio of *everyone* on the call.

**Pro-Tip:** Don't worry about typing perfectly. Just jot down short, messy bullet points while the audio dictation runs in the background.

---

## 3. The Magic Moment: AI Enhancement
When your meeting is over, your notes might look messy. 
Simply click **"Mark as done ✓"** in the bottom left corner. 

Clarify will instantly lock the screen and display an **Enhancing...** animation. Behind the scenes, the AI reads your messy shorthand and your raw audio transcripts, and magically transforms them into a beautiful, professional, structured Markdown summary.

---

## 4. Collaborating & Video Calling
Click the **"Share"** button at the top to generate a secure, password-protected link for your team.

*   **Real-Time Sync:** When a colleague joins via your link, you will see a colored Avatar bubble appear. As they type, you will see their notes instantly appear on your screen.
*   **Spatial Video Calls:** Click the **"Video"** button to instantly turn the meeting into a secure video call. Your team's camera feeds will appear as floating glass bubbles that you can drag around the screen or pinch-to-zoom on your iPad!

---

## 5. Ask Clarify AI (The Magic Orb) ✨
Can't remember what was decided in a meeting last month? Don't search for it—just ask!

In the bottom right corner of your screen, you will see a glowing **Magic Sparkle Orb**. 
1. Click the Orb.
2. Ask a question out loud (e.g., *"What did we decide about the marketing budget?"*).
3. Clarify will search your entire meeting history and literally **speak the answer back to you** out loud!
